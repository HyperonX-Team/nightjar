# GOAL

Nightjar: a covert SOS app disguised as a calculator that silently alerts 3 trusted contacts with live GPS and a 30 second audio recording, with no in-app history and 24 hour server expiry.

## MVP success

- Real working calculator as the only visible surface.
- Hidden setup for PIN, 3 contacts, and permissions.
- Trigger via PIN + "=" or triple-tap.
- 30 second audio upload and 15 minute live location.
- SMS to 3 contacts with a coordinate link and an incident page link.
- Public incident page with map, trail, audio, and expiry.
- No in-app history. Server data expires after 24 hours.
- Android only, shipped as a sideloaded APK.

## Non-goals

iOS. Google Play distribution. Contact-side app. Push notifications. Deleting recipient SMS. Weather disguise. Analytics. Multi-language.

## Constraints

- Platform limits are documented honestly: FGS notification, microphone indicator, no SMS deletion.
- Legal review is required before distribution.
- No fabricated claims (R-36).
