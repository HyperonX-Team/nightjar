# Design audit 001, 2026-10-17

Scope: the Flutter app surfaces and the contact page. Mode: during and after
(AGENTS.md), so defects found while building were fixed at the time, and this
report records them with the open items that still need an owner decision.

Findings are numbered and cite the governing rule. Priority follows the tier:
Hard Gate is HIGH, Purpose-Gate is MEDIUM, Quality Locks are LOW.

## Findings

1. **HIGH, R-25 and R-32, fixed.** The contact page's map container carried
   `role="img"` while Leaflet inserts interactive zoom controls inside it.
   axe-core reported "nested-interactive". Fix: the container is now a plain
   `div`, and the coordinates line is the accessible source of truth. Evidence:
   axe violations went from 1 to 0, and all measured contrast pairs pass AA.

2. **HIGH, R-02, pass.** No em dash or en dash appears in any project file.
   Evidence: repository scan; the only matches were inside `node_modules`.

3. **HIGH, R-03, pass.** No horizontal overflow at 320 px on the contact page
   (`scrollWidth == innerWidth`), and no RenderFlex overflow in widget tests at
   320, 360, and 420 dp and at 200 percent text scale.

4. **HIGH, R-26, pass.** Every control has a real action: calculator keys
   compute, the long press opens the PIN gate, setup rows navigate, permission
   rows request or open settings, the contact sheet saves, the test alert sends,
   the contact page has one real `tel:` action. No placeholder controls and no
   dead navigation exist.

5. **HIGH, R-27, pass.** Data surfaces carry empty, loading, and error states.
   Contacts show empty slots, the permission rows show real platform states,
   the test alert has confirming, running, and summary phases, and the contact
   page has loading, active, ended, expired, offline, missing audio, missing
   location, and stale location states. All were exercised except the
   physical-device-only paths listed in the verification log.

6. **HIGH, R-36 and R-38, pass with one open item.** No fabricated claims,
   numbers, or testimonials exist. The Firebase project values are placeholders
   and labelled as such in `config.js` and `firebase_options.dart`. The launcher
   icon is a deliberate vector calculator glyph, but SPEC 22.1 requires owner
   approval before use and that approval has not happened. Open, needs owner
   decision.

7. **MEDIUM, R-23, open.** The launcher icon is an asset created from the
   design direction rather than from an explicit description by the owner. It
   is honest and neutral, but per R-23 it stays a proposal until approved.

8. **MEDIUM, R-31, pass.** Every visual decision has a written reason in
   `DESIGN.md` or a one line comment: the four color palette, the two typefaces
   and their roles, the 6 dp key radius against the 8 dp card radius, the single
   ledger rule motif, the absence of shadows, and the one accent moment per
   screen.

9. **MEDIUM, R-19, pass.** Motion is press states only in the app, at 90 ms
   with ease out. The contact page has no entrance or looping animation, and the
   loader animation is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion` (verified as
   computed `animation: none`).

10. **MEDIUM, R-09 and R-13, pass.** The only pill on the contact page is the
    Live badge, which marks a real state, and it has no glow or pulse. The
    status marks in setup are functional state icons, not decoration.

11. **MEDIUM, R-14 and R-05, pass.** The app uses a single-purpose screen
    rhythm (a setup stack with rows and one primary action), which is a
    deliberate choice for a utility, not a landing page template. There is no
    bento grid, no fake terminal, no pricing block, and no logo bar.

12. **LOW, R-11, pass.** Radius is used at two values only, 6 dp for keys and
    8 dp for cards, rows, buttons, and the map. Nothing is pill shaped except
    the real live badge.

13. **LOW, R-29, pass.** Paper, ink, key surface, and oxide. Status colors are
    functional and only appear on status text and marks. Measured contrast is in
    the verification log.

14. **LOW, R-06, pass.** IBM Plex Sans for UI and IBM Plex Mono for the
    calculator display and the coordinates, with the reason recorded: fixed
    width digits do not shift as values change. Both are bundled, no runtime
    fetch.

## Delivery Gate

Block 1, Hard Gate. All answers are "no" to the failure questions, with
evidence recorded in `docs/VERIFICATION.md`.

- No em dashes in any text. Repository scan, only matches in node_modules.
- No horizontal overflow on mobile. Browser check at 320 px plus widget tests.
- No unsourced statistics. No numbers appear outside real data and measured
  test output.
- No fictional testimonials, avatars, or people. None exist.
- No assets created without instruction or an honest placeholder. The icon is
  finding 7, open for approval.
- No dead navigation. Every row, button, and link has a real destination.
- No text below AA contrast. Every pair measured; minimum is 4.70:1.
- No non functional controls. Every control was exercised or is covered by a
  widget test.
- Empty, loading, and error states exist. Listed in finding 5.
- No generic FAQ. There is no FAQ.
- Keyboard access works. Focus rings exist on keys, buttons, inputs, and page
  links; the page was audited with axe; the calculator supports Enter and Space
  activation and focus traversal.
- No script patching of source. All source is written directly.
- One theme only, by design, so no broken second mode.
- The app was built, installed, and exercised on an Android emulator; the page
  was loaded and exercised in a browser. Every interactive element of the page
  was checked in the states above.
- No fabricated security, compliance, or performance claims. The README and
  setup guide state platform limits plainly.
- Direction exists (`DESIGN.md`) and the dials are declared: ENERGY 1, RHYTHM
  1, MOTION 1.
- No realistically styled fabricated content. The Firebase values are labelled
  placeholders.

Block 2, Purpose-Gate. No gradients, glow, glass, shadows, decorative arrows,
background grids, generic icons, fake terminals, or template animations exist.
Monospace is used for numeric readouts with a stated reason. Icons are
functional status marks, a back control, and a chevron affordance.

Block 3, Liveliness. Dials declared in DESIGN.md and held: ENERGY 1, RHYTHM 1,
MOTION 1. Each screen has one focal point: the display on the calculator, the
status title on the contact page, the primary action in setup. Whitespace is
structural, the accent appears once per surface, and the ledger rule repeats as
the identity motif. The Design Read was taken from DESIGN.md before building.

Block 4, Craftsmanship and Quality Locks. No decision rests on "the AI
default"; the palette and type come from the design direction. No interactive
element is dead. No section exists to fill a template. The UI holds in every
shipped state and breakpoint, and was exercised with a keyboard and with
axe-core. No fabricated evidence. The section rhythm matches RHYTHM 1. Radius is
not uniform pill shaping. CTAs are specific: "Send a test alert", "Call
emergency services", "Unlock", "Continue". No marketing buzzwords. The identity
would not survive a logo swap test failure, because the calculator face, the
ledger rule, and the oxide accent are specific to this product. The palette is
within the cap. It does not clone a known product. Every major decision has a
one line reason.

Gate result: PASS for the build. One open owner decision remains, the launcher
icon approval in findings 6 and 7. It is an asset approval question, not a gate
failure, and it is recorded rather than silently shipped as final.
